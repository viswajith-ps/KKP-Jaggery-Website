Surendran KK, Founder
The man behind the furnace

Surendran KK

Founder & Proprietor, KPP & Sons Pvt Ltd

For more than 70 years, Surendran KK has run KPP & Sons single-handedly — from sourcing the cane, to boiling it in open pans the traditional way, to personally delivering jaggery to the temples that have trusted his family for generations.

What started as a small, local supply has grown into a name recognised by over 50 temples and churches across Kerala, who rely on KPP & Sons for jaggery used in prasadam and daily rituals. Through it all, the process has stayed the same: unhurried, hand-tended, and answerable to one man's standards.

Adopt a jaggery plot, anywhere in the world.

Choose a patch of our sugarcane fields to call your own, follow it from planting to harvest, and receive the jaggery it produces — pressed, boiled, and shipped straight to your door.

Adopting a plot is a symbolic sponsorship of a section of our farm. It does not transfer legal ownership or title to any land — it's a way to follow, name, and receive the harvest of a patch of cane grown especially for you.

01

Choose your plot

Pick a patch of sugarcane land and give it a name. We mark it out and it's yours to follow for the season.

02

We tend it for you

Our team grows and cares for your cane through the season, the same way we have for 70 years.

03

Harvest & boil

Once ready, your cane is pressed and boiled in open pans, the traditional way — no shortcuts, no refining.

04

Shipped to you

Your jaggery is packed and shipped to your door, anywhere in the world.
